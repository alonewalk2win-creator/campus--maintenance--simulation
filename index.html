Two maintenance workflows

Use the simulator to explore how maintenance requests can be tracked and prioritized.

Manual Register

Paper-based tracking can make it harder to follow request status, deadlines, and technician workload.

  • Manual request tracking
  • Harder to identify overdue work
  • Manual prioritization
🗃️

Database-style Workflow

A structured workflow makes request priority, deadlines, and staff availability easier to review.

  • Organized request status
  • Priority and deadline tracking
  • Workload visibility
